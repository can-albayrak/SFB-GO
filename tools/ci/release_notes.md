## How to play (Windows)

1. Download the `SFB-GO-...-windows.zip` below and extract it anywhere (keep `steam_api64.dll` and `steam_appid.txt` next to the exe).
2. Run `SFB-GO.exe`. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher (the game is not signed): **More info → Run anyway**.
3. **Steam:** everyone has Steam open and signed in. Host: **HOST ON STEAM** in the Steam panel. Friends: **FIND STEAM GAMES** → JOIN, or accept the host's Steam invite (INVITE in the lobby).
4. **IP (no Steam needed):** Host: **Host Game**. Friends: the host's IP → **Join** (port 7777, UDP).

## What's new (v0.10.0)

- New map **Train Factory** (CS Online's dm_trainfactory, converted 1:1 with its textures and lighting) with 16 spawns spread over the hall. The Mall is shelved for now; Ice Yard is the default map.
- Ladders: hold forward and look up to climb, look down to go down, jump to let go.
- New class **Gambler**: Roll the Dice (Q). Jackpot (2x damage), Hot Hand (no reload, faster fire), Lucky Streak (full health, faster), All In (everyone sees everyone through walls), Cold Hands (half damage), Snake Eyes (10 health).
- Trickster's Swap Dart now also trades primaries and any carried airdrop weapon (pistols stay).
- Subtle hit direction arc around the crosshair when someone shoots you.
- Airdrop crates fall from under the roof on indoor maps.
