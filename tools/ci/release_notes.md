## How to play (Windows)

1. Download the `SFB-GO-...-windows.zip` below and extract it anywhere (keep `steam_api64.dll` and `steam_appid.txt` next to the exe).
2. Run `SFB-GO.exe`. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher (the game is not signed): **More info → Run anyway**.
3. **Steam:** everyone has Steam open and signed in. Host: **HOST ON STEAM** in the Steam panel. Friends: **FIND STEAM GAMES** → JOIN, or accept the host's Steam invite (INVITE in the lobby).
4. **IP (no Steam needed):** Host: **Host Game**. Friends: the host's IP → **Join** (port 7777, UDP).

## What's new (v0.12.0)

- **Maps:** new map **Snow Town** (a snowy village in the spirit of fy_snow: enterable houses with roof lookouts, a shop, a barn, courtyards with trucks, a big decorated pine in the square). **Ice Yard** got real textures, shipping containers, a frozen fountain, lamps, roofed sniper nests and a warehouse skyline. Train Factory is shelved.
- **New models:** Scout is a Barrett M82, Desert Eagle has its own (plain) model, Sticky Bomb is a C4.
- **Sticky Bomb:** Q throws it, Q again within 30 s sets it off (striped bar); it goes off by itself at the end.
- **Spray:** letting go of the trigger now restarts the recoil pattern (it used to carry on from where it stopped).
- **AK-47:** new sound and calmer kick. New sounds for Desert Eagle, LMG, Scout (shot + bolt), magazine reloads, launcher blasts, near-miss bullet whiz, empty-gun click, weapon draw, pickups, getting hit and bodies falling.
- **Abilities are ready again after you respawn.** Hitboxes about 15 % bigger. Swap Dart is much easier to land. Sonar tells you how many it found and marks Test Range dummies.
- **Nicks** are smaller and only show when the player is in sight.
- **Settings:** Audio section with Master and Effects volume. **Lobby:** the host picks the respawn time.

## What's new (v0.11.0)

- **New weapon models:** AK-47 (the Assault Rifle is now an AK-47 with a heavier, punchier shot: 18 damage every 0.13 s, still 6 bullets to kill), Glock 17 (Pistol), suppressed USP-S, Colt single-action Revolver, Double Barrel shotgun, MP5SD with suppressor, new Rocket Launcher and Grenade Launcher.
- **Hit feel:** headshot / leg / kill sounds, kill marker and view punch, instant hit marker, bullet impacts and blood on surfaces, a kick profile per weapon, bolt-action rifles drop out of the scope after a shot.
- **Back to the lobby** after the end screen instead of an automatic new match.
- **Sonar** now marks Test Range dummies too, the red covers the whole body and gun and is easier to see; decoys never copy it.
- **Test Range:** switching class always starts at full health (the low-health red edge no longer stays). Switching class ends a Ghost cloak.
